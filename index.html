Privacy Policy for Happy Puzzle World

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Happy Puzzle World is a preschool puzzle game designed for young children. We are committed to protecting children’s privacy and keeping the game experience simple and safe.

Information we collect

Happy Puzzle World does not require an account, sign-in, name, age, email address, profile, or other personal information from children.

The app does not include advertising, behavioral tracking, social features, or user-generated content.

Purchases

Happy Puzzle World offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock additional game content. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Google may process purchase and device information in accordance with Google’s own privacy policies and terms.

Analytics and tracking

Happy Puzzle World does not use advertising SDKs or child-directed tracking for marketing purposes.

Offline use

Gameplay is designed to work offline. An internet connection may be required for Google Play purchase and entitlement verification.

Children’s privacy

The game is intended for young children. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children through the app.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes.
